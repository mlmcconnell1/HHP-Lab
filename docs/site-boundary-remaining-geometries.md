# Historical scope reconciliation for the 61-site design

## Current study frame

The selected study contains **61 sites and 81 CoCs**, using the frozen 2024 footprint from the rebuilt 99% universe. Outcomes cover 2010–2024 with 2021 excluded, so each site can contribute 14 annual observations. CT-503, OR-504, and PA-512 are excluded consistently. This note updates the former “82 remaining geometries” screen for the current 81-CoC design; the retained 82-row screen below is a historical screening artifact, not the current sample roster.

The remaining assignment-specific cases in this note are TX-603 (El Paso), HI-501 (Honolulu), CA-613 (Imperial County), and CA-501 (San Francisco). The machine-readable [administrative-scope reconciliation](</home/matt/HHP-Output/site-boundary-history-audit/remaining/assigned-scope-reconciliation.json>) records primary sources, year recommendations, confidence, and limits. The original [82-CoC geometry screen](</home/matt/HHP-Output/site-boundary-history-audit/remaining/remaining-geometry-screen.json>) and [source-access audit](</home/matt/HHP-Output/site-boundary-history-audit/remaining/assigned-geocode-source-audit.json>) are retained as historical artifacts. Their sample sizes and blanket source-access status do not represent the current 81-CoC sample.

## Recommendation

For these four cases, use the **stable administrative count unit** for 2010–2020 and 2022–2024, with 2021 omitted by design. The primary records consistently identify the same whole county or consolidated city-county unit at the early and recent endpoints, and no merger, split, or transfer was found. This is a moderate-confidence administrative-scope recommendation. It does not assert that all annual HUD polygons are identical to the frozen 2024 polygons or that the complete annual e-snaps assignment history was recovered.

The 2023 HUD GeoCodes/PPRN report’s search-indexed rows provide CoC-to-geocode evidence for all four cases: TX-603 has El Paso city and El Paso County codes; HI-501 has the Honolulu code; CA-613 has El Centro and Imperial County codes; CA-501 has the San Francisco code. The report PDF URL is currently not directly retrievable (404), so these specific indexed rows were not archived as a parsed source. FY2024’s downloadable GeoCodes report is only a code inventory and does not prove each CoC’s selected set. For San Francisco, the official city map establishes the legal county scope, including the Farallon Islands.

## Preserved 82-CoC geometry screen and broader limits

The original geometry screen used 84 distinct CoCs from the threshold-0.99/site-eligible universe, then removed CT-503 and MA-505 to yield 82 rows. The current study instead excludes CT-503, OR-504, and PA-512, yielding 81 CoCs. Therefore the retained [82-row screen](</home/matt/HHP-Output/site-boundary-history-audit/remaining/remaining-geometry-screen.json>) is not the current membership roster: it includes OR-504 and PA-512 and excludes MA-505. Its measurements are still useful as a geometry screen but cannot be read as current sample certification.

For its 82 rows, the screen found no complete annual assigned-geocode set comparison and no blanket approval. Across the 82, every FY2023 polygon is topologically unequal to the frozen 2024 reference; most residual fractions are much smaller than the cases discussed above. The 2020 local screen also marks differing geometry as “substantive boundary compatibility not established.” The local 2019 matches came from the same live endpoint and are not independent historical evidence.

CA-501 provides a useful second geometry pattern: its 2023 symmetric difference is just 3.98 m² (`3.24e-8` of union), while its 2022 geometry is 0.4686% smaller by area and has a maximum bounds delta of about 55.8 km. The [official San Francisco city map](https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2024-06/Citywide_Neighborhood_Map_July_2022%5B1%5D.pdf) confirms Farallon Islands are within the legal city-county boundary. It does not by itself prove the 2022 HUD polygon omitted those islands, so that remains a plausible, unverified feature-level explanation.

MA-515 (Fall River) is also retained in the old 82-row screen: its 2022 area difference is 2.3248% with a 535.6 m bounds delta, then its 2023 shape is effectively coincident with the frozen reference (1.15 m² symmetric difference). This suggests a year-specific geometry correction but is not a substitute for annual assignment evidence. MA-505 is outside that screen and has a separate merger reconciliation; see [the historical research notes](hhplab_historical_coc_research.md) and [the current 2024 PIT/HIC audit](site-pit-hic-2024-input-audit.md).

The frozen reference is the B2024 live ArcGIS snapshot, SHA-256 `93eec38a508d5faf9589edded506613851a14dd0112025314e5359e468f9d6ac`, captured in 2026 and attributed to FY2024. It is not the preserved FY2024 annual ZIP. The source archive metrics are in `archives-boundaries-epsg6933.json`, exact 2023 overlays in `archives-boundaries-2023-full-overlay.json`, and local 2020/2022 statuses in `local-boundaries.json`, under `~/HHP-Output/site-boundary-history-audit/`. Area and bounds comparisons describe only supplied shapes; they do not establish annual CoC membership, HUD e-snaps selections, program eligibility, or actual service-area definitions.
